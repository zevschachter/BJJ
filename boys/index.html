Why Jiu Jitsu

Train hard. Show up to shiur clean.

Jiu Jitsu is grappling, not striking. No punches, no kicks to the head. You learn to control someone bigger than you, escape bad positions and end a fight without trading blows. That means no black eye at Shacharis and no split lip in shiur. And because there's far less head trauma, it's a sport you can keep doing for the next 40 years, not just this one.

How It Works

No gym. No travel. No excuses.

Classes come to your yeshiva: a free shiur room, the dining room after seder, the park down the block. I bring the mats and the gear. You show up in something you can move in. Never trained before? Perfect. Most guys haven't.

Safety

Tough, not reckless.

Every class starts with a full warm-up and teaches technique before anything live. You'll roll with guys your size and level, always supervised. Tap early, tap often, train tomorrow.
