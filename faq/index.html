Questions

Good to know.

Is this BJJ for yeshiva students in Jerusalem?

Yes. The Yeshiva Grappling League runs Brazilian Jiu Jitsu and self-defense classes for English-speaking yeshivot across Israel, with open mats that bring bochurim from different yeshivot together.

Does it interfere with seder?

Never. Every class and open mat is scheduled around seder: night seder alternatives, Friday mornings, or bein hazmanim.

What does the yeshiva need to provide?

Just the space. A nearby park, a shiur room that's free for the hour, or a dining room after seder. I bring the mats and all the equipment.

How big are the classes?

3–6 bochurim per class.

Do bochurim need experience?

No. Classes are beginner-friendly, technique-first, and fully supervised. Sparring is matched by size and experience.

Is Brazilian Jiu Jitsu safe for yeshiva bochurim?

Yes. Jiu Jitsu has no punches or kicks, so there's far less head trauma than boxing or MMA. Every class starts with a warm-up, teaches technique before anything live, and all rolling is supervised and matched by size and experience.

Will my bochurim come to shiur with bruises or a black eye?

No. Jiu Jitsu is grappling, not striking. Nobody gets hit in the face, so nobody walks into shiur with a black eye or a split lip.

Why Jiu Jitsu instead of Krav Maga, boxing or MMA?

Jiu Jitsu teaches you to control someone bigger than you without trading blows. It's practical self-defense you can train hard several times a week without injuries that get in the way of learning.

Do the bochurim need any experience?

No. Most guys have never trained. Classes start from the basics.

What should they wear?

Something they can move in: a T-shirt or rashguard and athletic pants or shorts. No shoes on the mat.

Where do classes take place?

At your yeshiva: a free shiur room, the dining room after seder, or a park nearby. I bring the mats and all the gear.

Is this for Shana Alef, Shana Bet, or older bochurim?

All of them. Classes are grouped by experience, and we run open mats where bochurim from different yeshivot train together.

How much does it cost?

The first class is free. After that, pricing depends on group size and how often you train. Message me for details.
