Who I Am

A few years ahead of your bochurim, on the same derech.

I learned in yeshiva in Eretz Yisroel for 4 years and spent a year as a dorm counselor at Waterbury Mesivta, where I gave a chabura on Pirkei Avos and trained at Nova União. I know what the year in Israel is. I know what dorm energy on a Motzei Shabbos looks like.

I've trained in martial arts since I was 14: Krav Maga, Muay Thai, MMA, and Brazilian Jiu Jitsu. Today I train with ATOS Jerusalem and the Israeli national team.

I'm not an onlooker. I'm a ben Torah who happens to compete at the highest level. I built this program for the guy I was: the one who needed this outlet and didn't have it.

Why It Works

Mussar on the mat.

Bochurim carry real energy and real stress. Jiu Jitsu channels both into discipline, humility, and physical confidence with a structured, supervised setting inside your own walls. You tap, you learn, you get back up. No co-ed gym. No outside environment. No travel.

And for your program, it's something to tell parents: a weekly BJJ class run by a competitor training with the Israeli national team. That goes in your marketing materials, not just your schedule.
